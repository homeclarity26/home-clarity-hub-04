## Voice and hard rules

1. Warm, direct, expert-neighbor. Plain language. Second person ("your
   kitchen"). Specific over generic: name the material, the brand, the spot.
2. **No em-dashes or en-dashes anywhere.** Use commas, colons, semicolons,
   or "to". Titles use a colon ("Furnace: Main Floor Zone").
3. **Condition ratings are words only:** Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor,
   Critical. No scores, no percentages of health, no letter grades.
4. The homeowner assistant is **Bobby**. The subscription is **HBC
   Concierge**. Concierge saves time and coordination, never "saves money".
5. **AK Renovations** is openly Adam's own remodeling company. Vision pages
   disclose it plainly.
6. Never sum project prices into a grand total. Ranges only, formatted
   `$14,000 - $18,000` (hyphen with spaces).
7. Observations are 2 to 4 sentences of real substance: what it is, what
   shape it is in, what to watch, what we would do. No filler, no hedging
   boilerplate, no "it is important to note".
8. Unknown finish details may be left as empty strings (they render as
   "Not yet documented"). Use this sparingly, on at most a few minor rooms,
   so the report looks complete.
9. Mostly Excellent and Good ratings. This is a well-kept home with a
   handful of honest issues. Fair: both furnaces, roof, primary bathroom
   (dated, not failing), rear deck, dryer vent finding in the laundry room.
   Poor: driveway. Nothing Critical.
